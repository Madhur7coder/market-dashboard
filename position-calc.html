How to use: Set your account equity and the % of equity you'll risk on this trade. Enter the planned entry and stop-loss prices — the calculator returns the exact share count that caps your downside at the chosen risk amount. The 2-stop / 3-stop tabs split the position into staggered exit tranches; the R:R panel shows price targets and dollar P&L at common reward multiples (1R, 1.5R, 2R, 3R, 4R, 5R).
